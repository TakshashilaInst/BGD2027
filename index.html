About the Dialogue

An Invitation to Conceive the Future of
Technology, Innovation and Strategy in India

Bengaluru has a story to tell the world. In technology, bioeconomy, defence, security, economics and geopolitics, it has carved out distinct approaches that can help the world address some of the biggest challenges of our times. No other city so viscerally embodies the intersection of technology and ambition.


Now, for the first time, Bangalore Global Dialogue aims to become an independent platform for global conversations on ideas for the future: connecting innovators, policymakers, industry captains and thought leaders in a carefully curated experience in Taj Bangalore, a stone's throw away from Kempegowda International Airport Bengaluru.


It will be future-oriented, favouring deliberations that seek solutions for the next decade rather than problem scoping. Being a global city, the convening will bring together the rockstars of the policy, technology and business spheres from across the world.


Bangalore Global Dialogue will be held in partnership with the Raisina Dialogue 2027.

Programme Formats

A Mela of Ideas, Debates
& Discoveries

There will be three partner tracks for diplomacy, business & university, alongside the main tracks. Session formats will include brainstormers, panchayat-style discussions, sabhas, simulations & war games, purva paksha debates, UNSC-style roundtables, and more. While there will be keynotes, the Dialogue will encourage active participation of all attendees.

Brainstormers

Research-backed position papers and interactive discussions that are both grounded and forward-looking, drawing out the collective intelligence in the room.

Panchayat-style Discussions

Inspired by India's village deliberative traditions. A facilitator guides, but every voice in the room is given weight. Collective thinking in action.

Sabha

An open assembly format where participants deliberate on critical questions together, arriving at shared insights through structured dialogue.

Simulations & War Games

Participants, divided into groups, craft strategic moves addressing specific scenarios. Strategies are projected live, creating a mosaic of global complexity.

Purva Paksha Debate

Reviving the Indian scholarly tradition of articulating the strongest version of an opposing view before critiquing it — producing debates where participants listen deeply.

UNSC-style Roundtables

High-level closed roundtables modelled on Security Council deliberations, bringing together senior decision-makers to deliberate on the most pressing global challenges.

Conference Themes

Six Key Themes

01

High-Technology Geopolitics

Semiconductors, AI, quantum computing — how the race for technological supremacy is reshaping global power.

02

Innovation

Startups, deep-tech ecosystems, and the role of private enterprise in driving national and global competitiveness.

03

Defence & Military Affairs

Emerging military technologies, doctrine, and the future of warfare in an era of multi-domain conflict.

04

Bioeconomy

Bioeconomy, synthetic biology, and the intersection of life sciences with global strategic competition.

05

Geoeconomics & Geostrategy

Trade, sanctions, supply chain resilience, and the strategic use of economic power to shape global outcomes.

06

Astropolitics

Space as a strategic domain, satellite constellations, orbital deterrence, remote sensing, and geospatial intelligence.

Graduate certificate programmes in Advanced Public Policy, Defence & Foreign Affairs, and Technology Policy. Over 8,000 alumni across four continents including professionals, academics, civic leaders, and government officials.

Policy Research

Research spanning Advanced Biology, High-Tech Geopolitics, Strategic Studies, Advanced Military Technologies, Geospatial Research, Indo-Pacific Studies, and Economic Policy. Over 40 research staff in Bangalore and a network of 100+ adjunct scholars worldwide.
